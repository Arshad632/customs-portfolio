Classification memo

Sample
Product
Double-wall stainless steel bottle, 750 ml, vacuum between the walls, screw lid
Candidate headings
7323 (table and household articles of iron or steel) vs 9617 (vacuum flasks and other vacuum vessels, complete)
  1. GRI 1: classification starts from the heading texts and the section and chapter notes.
  2. Heading 9617 names the product by what it is: a complete vacuum vessel. A heading that describes the article specifically wins over one that describes it by material.
  3. The notes to Section XV exclude articles of Chapter 96, so heading 7323 can't take it.
  4. The vacuum layer is the deciding fact. A single-wall or non-vacuum steel bottle would go to 7323 instead, so the supplier spec sheet confirming the vacuum is kept on file.

Result: HS 9617.00. Evidence held: supplier spec sheet and product photos showing the vacuum construction.
